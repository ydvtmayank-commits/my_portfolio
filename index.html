CORE SPECIALTIES

What Drives My Engineering Pulse

The two primary mechanical domains where my passion, study, and future ambitions intersect.

AUTOMOTIVE & ENGINES

Automotive Engineering, Diagnostics & Custom Fabrication

Cars are the ultimate symphony of mechanical engineering—combining fluid dynamics, thermal cycles, electrical control, and chassis mechanics into one living machine.

  • Engine Diagnostics & Teardown: Studying 4-stroke cycles, valve timings, piston assemblies, and spark/fuel distribution.
  • Powertrain & Transmission: Manual gearboxes, differentials, clutch assemblies, and torque delivery.
  • Chassis & Suspension Dynamics: Double wishbone, MacPherson struts, anti-roll geometry, and structural rigidity.
  • Car Repair & Restoration: Practical troubleshooting of brake systems, cooling radiators, alternators, and steering linkages.
  • Future Ambition: Building custom project cars, go-karts, and exploring lightweight hybrid powertrains.
#InternalCombustion #ChassisDynamics #Fabrication #AutomotiveRepair
PRECISION MECHANISMS

Firearms Mechanics, Ballistics & Defense Technology

Firearms represent some of the most rigorous tolerances, extreme pressures, and instantaneous kinetic energy transfers in mechanical engineering.

  • Kinematic Cycle of Operation: Feeding, chambering, locking, firing, unlocking, extracting, ejecting, and cocking.
  • Operating Systems: Direct impingement, short-stroke & long-stroke gas pistons, roller-delayed blowback.
  • Internal & External Ballistics: Chamber pressure curves, rifling twist rates, projectile aerodynamics, and recoil mitigation.
  • High-Tolerance Metallurgy: High-tensile steel grades, chrome-moly barrels, nitriding, heat treatments, and CNC tolerances.
  • Engineering Respect: Studied strictly from mechanical science, design ingenuity, and defensive engineering perspectives.
#PrecisionMachining #GasSystems #Kinematics #Metallurgy
